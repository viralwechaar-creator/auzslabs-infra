From a table getting sat, to the kitchen finding out, to the bill printing right — one account, no paper tickets, no separate stock spreadsheet. The same system for a café, a restaurant, a bar, a bakery or a hotel's own food and beverage counter.

What cafés struggle with
Paper tickets and shouting across the counter

An order written on paper can get lost, misread or forgotten between the till and the kitchen.

No idea what is actually running low

Ingredient stock kept in a notebook, if at all — you find out you are out of something when a customer orders it.

Everything a café needs, in one account
POS
Tables, KOT & kitchen display

A floor plan by section, kitchen tickets the instant an order is sent, and a kitchen display that moves through new, preparing and ready.

Inventory
Ingredient stock & reorder alerts

Recipes deduct ingredient stock automatically as orders go through, with a reorder forecast built off real usage.

Billing
GST invoices & day settlement

Every bill numbered and tax-broken-down automatically; close a day once it is settled, reopen it if something needs fixing.

QR Ordering
Self-order & your own mini-site

A QR code on every table, and a public page for your café that doubles as the ordering menu.

CRM
Regulars, loyalty & feedback

See who is coming back and who has gone quiet, run a coupon, and collect a star rating right off the receipt.
