Pricing FAQs

Ask us a question

How does Basic pricing work?

Choose AUZsLedger, AUZsStyle, Salon Console, or a combination. Tell us about your business. We verify your requirements and send a quote for the apps you selected.

Is a website included with a Basic app?

AUZsStyle and Salon Console can be quoted on their own. Retail storefronts and salon websites have separate charges, which are listed separately in your quote.

How many user accounts come with each storefront plan?

Pro includes one user account. Advanced includes five staff accounts. Enterprise includes unlimited user accounts. Ask us about your team setup if you need help choosing.

Are GST and other fees included in the displayed prices?

The monthly prices shown exclude GST. If your setup needs a paid theme, a domain purchase, custom work or third-party services, we’ll confirm any separate charges before you proceed.

What does WhatsApp automation include?

Advanced and Enterprise include support for connecting and configuring WhatsApp automation. Available messages depend on your provider setup and approved templates. Provider usage charges may apply.

Can I connect a domain I already own?

Yes. Tell us where your domain is registered and we’ll confirm the setup and DNS changes needed to connect it. Domain registration and renewal charges depend on your provider.

Can I change or cancel my plan?

Contact us about your subscription. We’ll confirm any price or access changes before switching your plan. Review our refund and cancellation policy for details.

Can I try the apps before requesting a quote?

Yes. The live demo lets you explore AUZsStyle, AUZsLedger and Salon Console with sample data.
