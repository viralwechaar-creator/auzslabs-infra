Questions

Are these the final prices?

They are indicative starting prices per business per month, before GST. After you send a request we check your setup and send a written quote. That quote is what you pay.

Is there a setup cost?

There is a one-time onboarding fee for a new business (currently shown on your quote). AUZsMob and AUZsScan are quoted without GST or a setup fee.

Can I pay yearly?

Yes. Yearly plans are priced at ten months. Tell us on the request page and your quote will show it.

Can I add or remove products later?

Yes. Add a product from your account any time and we send an updated quote. Your data stays in place.

What about custom software or hardware?

Studio projects, website builds, printers and scanners are quoted per project. Contact us and describe what you need.
