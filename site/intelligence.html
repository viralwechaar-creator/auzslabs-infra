AUZslab Intelligence

Your business generates data.
We help you understand it.

Every sale, purchase, expense, shift and stock movement already lands in one place. Intelligence turns it into clear answers and better decisions.

From activity to understanding

Traditional software says "here are your numbers". AUZslab shows what changed, why it matters, and what to look at.

01
Business activity

Bills, purchases, expenses, attendance, stock moves.

02
Connected data

One system, so a sale already knows its stock, cost and staff member.

03
Insight, then action

Alerts and summaries that point you to what needs a look today.

What it looks at

Six views of one business.

Sales
  • Today against yesterday
  • Busy hours and quiet ones
  • Best and slowest sellers
  • Revenue by channel and payment
Inventory
  • Low and sold-out stock
  • Items close to expiry
  • Reorder points
  • Wastage and stock changes
Profit
  • Margins by item, from recipe costs
  • Expenses against revenue
  • Discounts, voids and refunds
  • Cash expected in the drawer
Staff
  • Sales per biller
  • Profit per staff member (AUZsMob)
  • Attendance and hours
  • Every void and approval, logged
Customers
  • New, regular and lapsed customers
  • Visit and spend history
  • Dues to collect
  • Birthdays and memberships ending
Operations
  • Kitchen and order timings
  • Tasks running late
  • Bills waiting to sync
  • One activity log for the whole day
