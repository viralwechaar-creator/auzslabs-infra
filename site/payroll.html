Payroll

Pay your team
from the same login.

A monthly rate per staffer, hours pulled straight from their own clock-ins, and one button to mark a month paid — no separate spreadsheet to keep in sync.

Included
Attendance, already tracked

Staff clock in and out on their own login — the same shift log the rest of the till already keeps, not a second system to maintain.

Included
A rate per person

Set a monthly rate for each staffer once; hours worked that month are computed automatically from their real clock-ins.

Included
Adjustments, with a note

Add a bonus or a deduction against any month, with a short note kept alongside it for when you look back.

Included
Mark paid, keep a record

One button turns a computed month into a payslip — re-marking the same month later updates it instead of piling up duplicates.

Included
Owner-only, by design

Salary data is kept off every other device on the account, not just hidden from the screen — a cashier or manager never gets it on their device at all.

Included
Payslip history

Every month you've marked paid, kept in one list, exportable per staffer whenever you need it on hand.
