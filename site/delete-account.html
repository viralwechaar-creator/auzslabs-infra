Delete your account.

How to delete your AUZslab account and what happens to your data.

Delete your account yourself
  • Sign in at auzslab.in/signup.html and open Account.
  • Scroll to Delete account and follow the steps. If your account has a password you will be asked for it.
  • You can also delete your account from Settings inside the POS, Payroll, Accounting and AUZsMob apps.
Or ask us to delete it

Email helloauzslab@gmail.com from the address on your account, saying you want your account and data deleted. We reply within 2 working days and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What happens
  • Your sign-in stops working straight away.
  • Your business data is deleted or anonymised within 30 days.
  • We keep only what the law requires, such as billing and tax records.
  • Encrypted backups are replaced on a regular cycle, so deleted data leaves them within a few weeks after that.
  • Deleting is permanent. Export your data first from the Back up / Export option in your app if you need a copy.
Staff accounts

A staff member with a username and PIN is removed by the business owner (Staff, then turn off or remove the login). If you are staff and want your personal details removed, ask the business owner or email us.
