What most POS software gets wrong for clothing
One stock number per product isn't enough

A "T-shirt" isn't one thing to stock — it's a dozen size/color combinations, each running low on its own schedule.

Trial room chaos, returns on paper

What a customer took to try on isn't a sale yet, but most tills have no way to tell the two apart — and a return means undoing a receipt by hand.

Built specifically for this
POS
Size & color billing

Every product's stock tracked by size and color, not one flat number — pick the exact variant a customer wants, or scan its own barcode.

POS
Trial room queue

Garments a customer's trying on go into their own queue, kept apart from actual sales until they decide.

Billing
Returns & exchanges

Return or exchange a sold variant against its original invoice — stock restocks or swaps automatically either way.

Inventory
Purchase entry, per variant

Enter a vendor bill straight against the exact size/color rows it restocks — not a flat "add stock" number.

Inventory
Reorder forecasting

A suggested reorder quantity built off which specific size/color is actually selling, not a guess.

Zero risk, try it first

Sell a size/color variant in a live account, not a demo reel.

A live, shared account seeded with sample size/color stock — sell a variant, put something in the trial room, try a return.
