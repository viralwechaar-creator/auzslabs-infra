Privacy policy.

Last updated 4 October 2026 · Version 2. What we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights. Written to follow India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Who we are and the two roles we play

AUZslab (“we”, “us”) provides business software for shops and small businesses in India: billing and point of sale, QR ordering, payroll, accounting, mobile shop and repair, website builder and related tools. This policy covers our website (auzslab.in), the accounts you sign in with, and the apps you use at auzslab.in and at your shop’s own address (shopname.auzslab.in).

We play two different roles, and the rules differ:

  • For our own visitors and account holders (people who contact us, sign up, or sign in as an owner or staff member) we decide why and how your personal data is used. Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) we are the data fiduciary for this data.
  • For the business data a shop puts into our software (its customers, orders, staff, payroll, stock, accounts) the shop decides what is collected and why; we only store and process it to run the service for that shop. We are the shop’s data processor. The shop is responsible for its own notices and for getting any consent it needs from its customers and employees.

If you are a customer or employee of a shop that uses AUZslab, see “Shop customers and employees” near the end of this policy.

What we collect

What you give us

  • Contact form and messages to us: your name, business name, phone number or email, and what you write.
  • Account sign-up: name, email address, phone number, and a password. We never store your password itself, only a one-way scrambled version (a hash) that cannot be turned back into the password.
  • Billing details: business name, GST number and billing address if you give them, and the plan you chose. Card, UPI and net-banking details are handled by our payment provider (Razorpay); we do not see or store your full card or UPI details, only the payment status, amount and reference.
  • Anything you send to support, including screenshots.

If you sign in with Google, Apple or your phone

  • Google or Apple tell us your name, email address and a unique ID, and whether the email is verified. Apple may give us a private relay email instead of your real one if you choose that.
  • For phone sign-in we use your phone number and a one-time code sent by SMS.

What we collect automatically

  • Technical and security data: IP address, browser and device type, the time of sign-ins, and a list of the devices where you are signed in (so you can sign a lost phone out).
  • Error reports: when something breaks in our apps, a technical report (the page, browser, and error message) is sent to our error-monitoring service so we can fix it. We do not record your screen.
  • Notifications: if you allow browser or phone notifications, a notification address for your device.
  • Location: only when an employee clocks in from their phone at a workplace where the owner has turned on a location check, and only at that moment. We do not track location in the background.

Business data you store in the software (we process it for you)

  • Customers, orders, bills, bookings, repair jobs, stock, vendors and dues.
  • Staff and payroll records: names, phone numbers, attendance, salary details, bank account details, PAN, PF/ESI numbers and similar.
  • Accounting and GST records.
  • Photos and files you upload, such as bills, logos, and ID proofs a shop collects when it buys a used phone.

Some of this is sensitive. Please store only what you need, and do not upload full Aadhaar numbers or Aadhaar card images unless the law allows it for your purpose and you handle them as the law requires.

Why we use it
  • To create and run your account and provide the features you use.
  • To take payment and issue invoices, and to keep the records the law requires.
  • To keep the service secure: preventing fraud, abuse and unauthorised access, and limiting repeated login attempts.
  • To give support and to find and fix faults.
  • To meet legal obligations and to respond to lawful requests from authorities.
  • To tell you about changes to the service, and, only if you agree, about offers and new products. You can say no, or change your mind, at any time.

We rely on your consent, or on the other lawful uses the DPDP Act allows (for example, doing what is needed to provide the service you asked for, and complying with the law).

What we do not do: we do not sell or rent your personal data, we do not show advertising, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not use your business data to train AI models or for any purpose other than providing your service. We do not read your business data except when needed to give you support you asked for, to keep the service secure, or when the law requires.

Cookies and similar storage

We use only what is needed to make the service work: a sign-in session, your language and display choices, your business colour, and data stored in your browser so the apps keep working without internet (entries you make offline are kept on your device until they sync). We do not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when a page loads. Our staff apps load an error-monitoring script from Sentry. You can clear stored data in your browser settings, but this signs you out, and entries you made offline that have not synced yet can be lost, so sync first.

Who we share it with

We share personal data only with the service providers we need to run AUZslab, and only for that purpose. They are bound by their own terms to protect it:

  • Cloud hosting provider: runs our servers and database, where all data is stored.
  • Backblaze: stores backups of our database.
  • Resend: sends our emails, such as email verification and password resets (your email address and the message).
  • Razorpay: processes online payments. Its own privacy policy applies to the payment details you give it.
  • Sentry: receives technical error reports.
  • Google and Apple: if you choose to sign in with them; Google also serves fonts on our pages.
  • SMS provider: sends one-time sign-in codes if you use phone sign-in.

We may also disclose data if the law or a court or authority requires it, to protect our rights or the safety of others, or as part of a merger or sale of the business (in which case we will tell you and the same protections will apply). Within a shop, the owner and staff can see the business data the owner’s role settings allow.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored on servers we run with a cloud provider. Some of our service providers listed above (for example, Resend, Sentry and Backblaze) may process data on servers outside India. By using AUZslab you understand your data may be processed outside India, subject to the DPDP Act and any restrictions notified under it.

How long we keep it
  • Account details: while your account is open, and for up to 30 days after you close it or ask us to delete it.
  • A shop’s business data: while the shop’s account is active. After closure we keep a download of the data available for 30 days, then delete it.
  • Invoices, payment and tax records: as long as tax and company law require, generally six to eight years.
  • Payroll and accounting history: these records are permanent by design so that figures can always be traced. We keep them as the law requires and, where possible, remove or anonymise personal details.
  • Security and audit logs: at least 180 days.
  • Contact-form messages: until your question is answered and for up to 12 months afterwards.
  • Error reports: for the limited period set by our error-monitoring service (typically about 90 days).
  • Backups: data you deleted can remain in our backups until they expire, normally within six months. It is not used for any other purpose and is not restored except to recover from a failure.
Security

We protect your data with HTTPS for all connections, hashed passwords, role-based access, separation between each business’s data enforced in the database, the ability to sign out any device, tamper-resistant audit history for payroll and accounting, regular backups, and limits on repeated login attempts. No system is perfectly secure. If you think your account has been compromised, tell us straight away at helloauzslab@gmail.com.

If a breach affects your personal data in a way the law requires us to report, we will inform you and the authorities (including the Data Protection Board of India and CERT-In) as the law requires.

Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and who we share it with;
  • ask us to correct or complete it, or to erase it (except what we must keep by law);
  • withdraw a consent you gave us, as easily as you gave it;
  • have your grievance heard and resolved; and
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights for you if you die or become unable to.

To use a right, email helloauzslab@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request”, from the email address on your account so we can confirm it is you. We will reply within 7 days and complete the request within 30 days, or sooner if the law requires. You can also delete your own account yourself: use “Delete my account” in the POS, Payroll or Accounting app, or the Account page on our website. Deleting signs you out of every device and stops sign-in immediately, and we remove your personal data as described under “How long we keep it”. A shop’s business data can be closed only by the shop owner.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India once you have first raised the issue with us.

Children

AUZslab is a business tool and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

Shop customers and employees

If you are a customer of a shop that uses AUZslab (for example, you ordered by QR code or had a repair), or an employee whose attendance and payroll are managed with AUZslab, the shop is responsible for your data and decides how it is used. Please contact the shop first; each shop’s own privacy notice is available at its address (shopname.auzslab.in/privacy.html). We help shops respond to your requests and we handle your data only as the shop instructs us, as described in this policy.

Links, changes and updates

Our site and apps link to other services (such as Razorpay, Google, Apple and WhatsApp) that have their own privacy policies. We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version. For changes that matter, we will tell you by email or inside the app before they take effect.
