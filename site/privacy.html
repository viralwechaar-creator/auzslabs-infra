Privacy policy.

Last updated September 2026. What we collect, why, and what we don't do with it.

What we collect

When you fill out our contact form, we collect your name, business name, phone/email and whatever message you send us — just enough to get back to you. If you become a customer, your business's operational data (orders, customers, inventory) lives on infrastructure we run for you, scoped to your account only.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We don't share it with advertisers. We don't read your customers' data for any purpose beyond running the software you asked us to run.

Cookies & tracking

This marketing site doesn't use third-party analytics or ad tracking. Any local storage used (like remembering you've already seen our "knock" prompt) stays on your device.

Third parties

We use standard infrastructure providers to keep the servers running. None of them get access to your business data beyond what's required to host it securely.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you or your business, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it (subject to what we're required to keep for billing/legal reasons). Just email us.
