Mobile retail & repair

Everything your mobile shop needs. One connected setup.

Manage repairs, device sales, scan-based stock and accounts in one place, with each staffer seeing only their own numbers. In Hindi or English, even offline.

What mobile shops struggle with
Stock nobody can actually trust

A notebook or a spreadsheet can't tell two identical phones apart — so a swapped or returned handset just disappears into the count.

No real picture of who did what

Multiple staff billing and repairing off one shared login means the owner can't see — and staff can see each other's — sales and work.

Everything a mobile retail & repair shop needs
Inventory
Every phone tracked by its own IMEI

Each handset is its own unit with its own IMEI/serial, condition and warranty — not just a number in a stock count. Accessories stay simple plain-quantity stock.

Repairs
A real repair-ticket workflow

Intake a device, log the issue, track status through to done, record parts used and take payment — a genuine job tracker, not a sticky note.

Staff isolation
Each staffer sees only their own

Sales and repair jobs are recorded under the staffer who made them, and enforced by the system — one staff member can't see another's numbers, full stop.

Admin view
The owner sees everything, always

Every staffer's sales, repairs and totals roll up for the owner/admin — full visibility at the top, isolation everywhere else.

Purchasing
Suppliers, purchases & stock movement

Record what came in and from whom, with low-stock alerts and a full movement log behind every change in stock.
