Overview

One platform, every part of the business.

AUZslab is a set of connected business apps: billing and point of sale, customers, stock, staff pay, accounts, and systems made for particular kinds of shop. They share one login and one set of records, so a sale made at the counter is already in the books, and a staff member's clock-in is already in the pay run.

One login

An owner signs in once and sees only the apps their plan includes. Staff get their own username and PIN.

Yours to shape

Names, fields, roles, menus, invoice look and the owner's own logo and colours can all be set up for each business.

Works with weak internet

The counter apps keep selling when the connection drops and send everything once it returns.

Each business gets its own private workspace and its own address. Their data stays theirs and can be backed up or exported at any time.

Products

What each product is.

Clients pick only what they need today and add more later. CRM, billing and inventory come inside AUZsPOS; they are not bought separately.

Restaurants, cafes and shops

AUZsPOS

The counter and kitchen in one till.

  • Order taking, GST billing, due and credit tracking
  • Table floor plan, kitchen order tickets and a kitchen screen
  • Token or queue display for counter service
  • Day close, day and kitchen reports, a build-your-own report
  • Customer profiles, loyalty points and coupons; ingredient stock, purchases and wastage
  • Staff roles and permissions; manager approval for discounts and refunds
  • Works offline and syncs later
Add-on to AUZsPOS

QR ordering

A QR code on every table. Guests open the menu on their own phone and order straight to the kitchen. It also gives the business its own public mini-website with menu, hours and location, and it can be switched off any time.

Staff and salary

AUZsPay

Staff clock in on their phone or a shared kiosk, even without internet, and the hours go into the pay run.

  • Salary structures, overtime, PF, ESI, PT and TDS as visible rules
  • Leave requests, advances, loans and expense claims
  • Payslips for staff; managers see only their own team
Accounts and GST

AUZsLedger

Proper double-entry accounts for businesses that need real books.

  • GST invoices, purchases, quotations, receipts and credit notes
  • CGST, SGST and IGST worked out; input credit tracking
  • Bank statement import and matching
  • Profit and loss, balance sheet, trial balance and ageing, with every figure traceable to its bill
Billing by barcode

AUZsScan

Point a phone at a barcode to bill it with GST, take payment and send the invoice on WhatsApp. Scan stock in the same way. A new barcode is entered once; every later scan fills in price, HSN and GST. It also works with a Bluetooth scanner.

Mobile shops and repairs

AUZsMob

Phones tracked by IMEI, accessory stock, purchases, repair job tickets and customer dues. Each staff member sees only their own sales; the owner sees everyone's, with a profit table. English and Hindi.

Clothing and footwear

AUZsStyle

A till for clothing and shoe shops: styles with colours and sizes, tag printing, scan to bill, returns and exchanges, and analytics on which style, size and colour sells first. It can also run the shop's own online store.

Money and control

AUZsFinance

For owners who want to see where the money is: money in and out, cash and bank, owner capital and loans, who owes you, repeating entries, bank statement matching and GST notes, on the same ledger.

Salons and spas

AUZslab Salon

The salon's own website, online booking, billing, clients, expenses and analytics in one console, with the salon's logo and colours. Bills go to customers as a WhatsApp link.

Made for you

Websites, online stores and custom software

A business website, an online store connected to the POS, or a system built around a particular way of working. These are scoped and quoted for each client.

How a client starts

From first request to first bill.

  1. The client sends a request. On auzslab.in they tick the services they want and fill in their business details. You can also send them the request page. Nothing is charged at this point.
  2. We check it and send a quote. AUZslab reviews the request and prepares a quote with the GST worked out. It reaches the client on WhatsApp with a link.
  3. The client reads the quote and pays. They open the link, read the terms, tick to accept, and pay online or in cash. The payment page also shows what is included.
  4. Their business goes live. After payment is confirmed their products are switched on, they get a GST bill, and they sign in at their own address.
  5. We help them set up. A setup person can sit with the client to add the menu or items, staff and details. The beginner manual walks owners through it step by step.

The quote is set by AUZslab for each client, so please do not promise a price. Send the client to the request page and we take it from there.

How it is used

The day-to-day, in plain steps.

Owner, first day

  • Sign in with Google, or email and password
  • Add business name, phone, address and logo
  • Add menu, items or services (a PDF menu can be read in automatically)
  • Add staff with a username and PIN
  • Make one test bill

Counter staff

  • Sign in on the Staff tab with username and PIN
  • Tap items, choose the table or takeaway, then Pay
  • Print the bill or send it on WhatsApp
  • Discounts, voids and refunds ask a manager for approval

End of day

  • Count cash in the register and close the day
  • Check the day report and the kitchen report
  • Owners see everything from the admin console

Staff pay

  • Staff tap Clock in and Clock out on their phone
  • At month end the owner checks the numbers and finalises
  • Staff see their payslip in the app

The full step-by-step manual is on the website under Resources → Learn. It is written for people who have never used business software.

Devices & internet

What it needs to run.

There is nothing to buy or install for the core apps. They run in a web browser, and they can be put on the home screen like an app.

ItemWhat is needed
Phone or tabletA current Android phone (Chrome) or iPhone/iPad (Safari). The apps can be added to the home screen from the browser. A small Android app is also available for some products.
ComputerAny Windows, Mac or Linux computer with a current Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. AUZsStyle can also be installed from Chrome or Edge to open like a program.
InternetOrdinary mobile data or Wi-Fi is enough. A first sign-in needs a connection. After that, AUZsPOS, AUZsMob, AUZsScan and AUZsPay clock-in keep working through short outages and send their data later. Accounts (AUZsLedger, AUZsFinance) and the online store need a connection to save and post.
Kitchen or token screenAny tablet or screen that can open a web page.
Barcode scanningThe phone camera works on Android and iPhone. A Bluetooth or USB barcode scanner that types like a keyboard also works. Camera scanning needs a clear, well-lit barcode.
PrintingBills print from the browser to any normal printer or save as PDF. Label printing (AUZsStyle tags) and receipt printers are set up with the business; please ask us before the client buys a printer so we can confirm the model.
Staff attendanceStaff use their own phone, or one shared tablet kiosk. Location is used only if the owner sets a workplace boundary.
Please ask us before promising a particular printer, scanner or old device. The easiest check is to let the client open the live demo on the phone or computer they will actually use.
Questions

What owners usually ask.

Can I try it first?

Yes. There is a live demo with sample data on the website, and it resets by itself. Open auzslab.in → Live demo for the address and login. It is for looking around only.

Is our data safe?

Each business has its own private workspace. Staff see only what their role allows, important changes need a manager's approval, and the owner can download a backup or export at any time.

What if the internet goes down?

The counter apps carry on selling and catch up when the connection is back. A small status line shows what is waiting to be sent.

Can it be made to fit our way of working?

Yes. Menus, items, roles, invoice look and many settings are the owner's to change. For anything bigger, AUZslab can build to order.

How do staff log in?

Each person has their own username and PIN, given by the owner. The owner can switch a login off or reset a PIN at any time.

Who looks after support?

Every app has a Help button that sends the details to our team. The client can also call or WhatsApp us.

What about commission and terms for partners?

Partner terms are agreed directly with AUZslab. They are not set out in this guide.
