Refund & cancellation policy.

Last updated October 2026. Simple rules for the one-year term, cancelling and getting money back.

1. Plain summary
  • Every plan is a minimum one-year agreement. You commit to one year of service when you subscribe.
  • Because the year is committed, fees already paid are not refunded if you stop early or change your mind.
  • You can choose not to renew. Tell us before the year ends and you keep using the apps until the end of the paid period.
  • We do refund money taken by mistake (charged twice, wrong amount) and, if we cannot deliver the service at all, the fees for the period we could not deliver.
2. Cancelling
  • Tell us by email at helloauzslab@gmail.com from the email on your account.
  • Minimum term: one year from your start date. If you pay monthly, the remaining months of the first year stay payable and your account may be paused if they are not paid.
  • Not renewing: after the first year there is no lock-in beyond the period you have paid for. Tell us before the renewal date and you will not be charged again; you keep using the apps to the end of the paid period.
  • We do not charge any extra cancellation fee.
3. Refunds
  • Committed year: subscription fees for the one-year term are not refundable once your account is set up and active, including if you stop using the apps.
  • Setup fee: not refundable once your business is set up.
  • If we fail: if the service cannot be used because of a problem on our side that we cannot fix, we refund the fees for the period we could not provide it.
  • Charged twice or by mistake: we refund the extra amount in full.
  • Free demo accounts are free: no payment, no refund.
4. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are returned to the same method you paid with (Razorpay refunds reach your bank in about 5 to 7 working days; UPI and bank transfers are returned to the account that paid). We process approved refunds within 7 working days.

5. How to ask

Email helloauzslab@gmail.com with the business name, the date and amount of the payment, and your payment reference. We reply within 2 working days.
