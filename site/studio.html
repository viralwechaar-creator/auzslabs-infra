Billing

Billed separately, based on the work.

Each project is priced from its own scope. There is no price list and nothing to add to a cart. Scope, price and payment milestones are written down and agreed before work starts. Studio work is billed on its own, apart from any standard AUZslab subscription you also use.

Questions
How much does it cost?

It depends on the work. We scope your project, give you a written quote, and bill it separately.

Do I own my data?

Yes. Your data sits in your own private workspace, kept apart from other businesses, and you can export it.

Can you add to it later?

Yes. New features are scoped and quoted the same way, as separate work.
