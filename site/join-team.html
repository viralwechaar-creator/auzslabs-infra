Careers

Work with us at AUZslab.

We are building business software for shops, cafes, salons and mobile stores. If you like helping small businesses get set up and succeed, tell us about yourself. We read every application.

How it works

1. Sign in with Google and save your profile. 2. Submit to join the team. 3. We review it and reply by phone or email -- you can always come back here to check your status. 4. Once approved, sign in at app.auzslab.in with Google, using the same email. Your own team dashboard opens there.

Roles: Salesman (find clients, run demos), Client setup (get new clients ready), Support (help clients after they go live), Manager (oversee the team).

Questions? helloauzslab@gmail.com
