Website

A site that looks like yours

Your logo, colours, fonts, hero photo, gallery, stylists and service menu on a fast public site. Edit every word and price from the admin.

Booking

Online booking with live slots

Clients pick services, a date and a time. You set opening hours, closed days, slot length and how many chairs share a slot.

Billing

Branded invoices over WhatsApp

Build a bill in seconds, send a short link on WhatsApp, and clients can print or save it as a PDF with your logo and footer.

Clients

Every client remembered

Bookings and bills build a client list automatically — visits, total billed and last visit, without a separate notebook.

Expenses

Rent, salaries and bills tracked

Log what goes out as easily as what comes in, so you always know what the month really earned.

Analytics

Numbers that make sense

Today's bookings, monthly billing and trends on one screen, built for a phone.

Staff

Staff logins with limited access

Your team signs in with their phone number to take bookings, make bills and send them on WhatsApp. They can't touch prices, the website, expenses or other people's bills.

Payroll

Clock-ins flow into AUZslab Payroll

Staff clock in and out from their phone. Hours become attendance in AUZslab Payroll, ready for the month's pay run.
