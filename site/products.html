08 · Your books

AUZs
Ledger

GST invoicing, purchases, stock, bank reconciliation and financial reports on a real double-entry ledger. Every figure drills down to the bill behind it.

  • CGST, SGST and IGST worked out for you, with input credit and GSTR-2B matching
  • One posting updates the ledger, stock and receivables together; posted entries are only ever reversed
  • Quotation, order, challan, invoice and receipt — part payments and credit notes settle against the right bill
  • Bank statement import and reconciliation to zero, bank charges posted in one tap
  • Trial balance, profit and loss, balance sheet and ageing — tap any figure to reach the bill behind it
