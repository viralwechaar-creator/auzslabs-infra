A little about Kiln

Our story

A neighbourhood bakery and café built around thoughtful baking, good coffee and the pleasure of slowing down.

The Kiln feeling

Warm bread.
Good company.

What matters to us

Made with care, never in a hurry.

At Kiln, the everyday things deserve attention: dough given time to rise, coffee made with care, seasonal bakes and a table where you can stay a little longer.

We’re here for early starts, slow afternoons, a quick takeaway and the small rituals that make a neighbourhood feel like home.

Come in for a coffee. Stay for something warm.

Built around a simple idea.
