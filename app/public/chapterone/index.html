Founder & Dreamer Chapter One, Jodhpur

Every cafe has a menu. Ours has a story. Chapter One began as a single page scribbled in the margins of a notebook — a quiet certainty that someday there’d be a room that smelled of fresh coffee, where people came in to begin something new.

She turned that one page into a place. No investors, no formula — just a life’s worth of wanting this, built cup by cup, table by table, in Jodhpur.

“This isn’t the whole book yet. It’s just the first chapter — and you’re part of writing the next one with us.”

— Founder, Chapter One
