Signing in and getting around

Everything works on a phone, tablet or computer. Nothing to install.

  1. Open app.auzslab.in (or your own business address).
  2. Owners and managers: choose Continue with Google, or use email and password.
  3. Staff: open the Staff tab and type the username and PIN your owner gave you.
  4. After sign in you see tiles for the apps your plan includes. Tap one to open it.
  1. Open the app address in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
  2. iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: tap the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Add each app you use from its own page, so its icon opens that app.

Owners: use Forgot password on the sign-in screen. Staff: ask your owner to reset your PIN from their dashboard under Staff. Never share a PIN.

Every app has Help & report a problem in its menu. It collects the details for you and sends a message to our team. You can also use the contact page.

For owners
Your first day, in order

Do these once. It takes about half an hour.

  1. Sign in and open the Admin console (or Settings in your app).
  2. Fill in business name, phone, address and opening hours.
  3. Upload your logo. The apps and printed bills use it.
  1. Open Menu (cafes and restaurants), Stock (AUZsMob) or Menu and prices (salon).
  2. Add a category, then add items with a name and price.
  3. Already have a PDF menu? In the admin console, Menu, tap Read menu file. Check the draft it makes, then create.
  1. Open your account page, then Staff.
  2. Tap Add staff and choose Username + PIN.
  3. Write down the PIN. It is shown once. The owner can reset it any time.
  4. Give each person only the permissions their job needs.

Open your app, add one item, take a payment and print or share the bill. Do it once as a test so staff see what customers will see. You can void a test bill afterwards if your permissions allow it.

Open Plan & account in any app to see your plan, renewal date and, if available, auto-renewal.

AUZsPOS
Taking orders and bills

For cafes, restaurants and shops.

  1. Tap Sell.
  2. Tap items to add them. Use the plus and minus to change quantity.
  3. Choose dine-in, takeaway or delivery. For dine-in, pick the table.
  4. Tap Pay, choose cash, UPI or card, and finish.
  5. Print the bill or share it on WhatsApp.

Tap Send to kitchen before payment. The kitchen screen shows the order. When the kitchen marks it ready, the cashier sees it.

AUZsMob
Mobile shops and repairs

Buy, sell and repair phones, with staff accountability. A short Hindi and English owner guide also exists, see the language switch inside the app.

  1. Tap New sale and search the item or IMEI.
  2. Confirm the selling price for this bill.
  3. Take payment: cash, UPI, card or credit.
  4. Share the receipt on WhatsApp.
  1. Tap Add purchase.
  2. Pick or add the item and the vendor.
  3. Enter quantity and purchase price. For phones, enter each IMEI.
  1. Tap New repair.
  2. Enter the customer, device, problem and advance paid.
  3. Update the status as the job moves along, and record parts and payments.

Each staff member sees only their own sales. The owner sees every staff member in Reports, with the profit table. The owner can also set a profit share under Settings.

AUZsPay
Staff attendance and salary

  1. Open AUZsPay on your phone.
  2. Tap the big Clock in button. Tap it again when you leave.
  3. No internet? It saves your punch with the real time and sends it later.
  1. Add each person under People with their salary.
  2. At month end open Payroll, check the numbers and finalise.
  3. Staff see their payslip under My pay.

Staff ask for leave under My leave. The owner or manager approves it.

AUZsLedger
Accounts and GST

  1. Open Sales, then New invoice.
  2. Pick the customer, add items and check the tax.
  3. Save as a draft or Post. Posted invoices get a number and cannot be edited, only cancelled.
  1. Open Sales, then Scan to bill. The first time, allow the camera when your phone asks.
  2. Tap Scan to sell and point at a barcode. Each scan adds the item; scan again to add one more. You can also type a name or code.
  3. A new barcode asks for the name, price, GST and stock once. After that, scanning fills it in.
  4. Choose cash, UPI, bank or credit, then Charge. The invoice is posted and stock goes down.
  5. Tap Send on WhatsApp, or print or save the PDF.

To bring stock in, switch to Add stock and scan what arrived. The same screen is also available as the small AUZsScan app at your business address, /scan.html.

Open Purchases and tap Scan invoice to read a PDF or photo, or enter it by hand. Always check the details before saving.

Open Financial reports for profit and loss, balance sheet and GST summaries. They are working papers for you and your accountant.

Good habits
Keep your business safe

  1. Give every person their own login. Never share one.
  2. Never write PINs on the counter.
  3. Remove a login the day someone leaves.
  4. Take a backup now and then from your account page.
  5. Use a long unique password for the owner login.
Staff login card
Give each staff member a card.

Print this, fill it in by hand and hand it over. The PIN is shown only once when you add a staff member.
