# Ideal customers

## Cafés, restaurants, bars, bakeries
Want fast billing, table QR ordering, kitchen tickets, and to know daily sales. Pain: paper bills, staff mistakes, no stock control, slow queues. Best fit: AUZsPOS + AUZsPOS QR, then AUZsPay.

## Salons and spas
Want bookings, customer history, staff attendance and a good-looking own website. Pain: phone-only bookings, no customer records. Best fit: AUZslab Salon, plus AUZsPay.

## Retail, bookstores, clothing
Want stock by item and size, quick billing with GST, customer list and offers. Pain: manual stock, wrong GST bills. Best fit: AUZsPOS, AUZsLedger.

## Mobile phone and repair shops
Want IMEI-wise stock, vendor purchases, repair tracking, profit per sale and staff accountability. Best fit: AUZsMob.

## Who decides
Usually the owner, often on a phone, often not technical. Prefers WhatsApp, short messages, Hindi-friendly English, a clear price and someone who sets it up for them.

## Not a fit (for now)
- Very large chains needing many integrations.
- Anyone expecting automatic Zomato/Swiggy order arrival (manual entry only today) — TODO verify before saying.
