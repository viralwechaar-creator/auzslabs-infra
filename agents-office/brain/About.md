# About AUZslab

AUZslab (auzslab.in) makes simple, reliable software for small businesses in India. One login gives a shop its billing, orders, customers, staff attendance and pay, accounts and bookings. We set it up for the client and look after it for them.

## What makes us different
- Built for Indian small businesses: GST, rupees, UPI, WhatsApp bills, Hindi and English where it matters.
- Works even when the internet is slow or off (billing and orders keep working and sync later).
- Each client gets their own business address, for example `yourshop.auzslab.in`.
- We do the setup and can build a specially designed system when a client needs something unique.
- A real person answers support.

## Tone of voice
Friendly, honest, practical. We never hype. We explain simply and never blame the customer.
