# Agents and pods

The Agents Office has 5 pods of 5 agents (25 agents). All of them only draft; the owner does every action.

## Engineering
AI Coding · AI Code Review · AI Bug Detection · AI Performance Optimization · AI UI/UX

## Operations & Security
AI DevOps · AI Server Monitoring · AI Database Monitoring · AI Cybersecurity · AI Backup & Disaster Recovery

## Product & Delivery
AI QA / Testing · AI Release / Deployment · AI Product Manager · AI Product Research · AI Documentation

## Customers & Growth
AI Customer Support · AI Customer Onboarding · AI Sales / Lead Generation · AI Marketing · AI SEO

## Business & Insight
AI Business Analytics · AI Data Analysis · AI Financial / SaaS Metrics · AI Internal Knowledge · AI Operations / Workflow

## The human team roles (for context)
CTO / Technical Lead, Full-Stack, Frontend, Backend and Mobile developers, UI/UX Designer, QA Tester, DevOps / Cloud Engineer, Cybersecurity Engineer, Product Manager, Sales Executive, Customer Onboarding Executive, Customer Support Executive, Customer Success Manager, Digital Marketing Executive, Content / SEO Executive, Finance / Accountant, HR / Admin, Legal / Compliance Consultant. The agents help these roles; they do not replace the owner's decisions.

## How monitoring-type agents work
Server, Database and Backup agents never connect to anything. The owner pastes logs, stats or settings into the task and the agent analyses what was pasted.
