# FAQ

## Does it work without internet?
Billing and orders in AUZsPOS and AUZsMob keep working offline and sync when the connection returns. Accounting needs a connection to post, but you can write drafts offline.

## Can my staff have their own logins?
Yes. Staff get a username and PIN. You decide what each person can open.

## Is GST handled?
Yes: GST on bills and GST reports in AUZsPOS and AUZsLedger. Your accountant should still check filings.

## Who sets it up?
We do. Setup is a one-time fee (see [[Pricing]]), and we help you load your menu or items.

## Can I get my data out?
Yes. Owners can download a full backup from their account.

## Can I share bills on WhatsApp?
Yes, with one tap.
