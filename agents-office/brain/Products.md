# Products

Names are written exactly like this.

## AUZsPOS
Billing / point of sale for cafés, restaurants, bars, bakeries, hotels, retail and more. Includes billing and invoicing, inventory, customers (CRM), kitchen display / KOT, purchases and vendors, memberships, tasks and a help desk. Works offline and syncs later. WhatsApp bill sharing. One admin console for menu, stock, staff and reports.

## AUZsPOS QR
Customers scan a QR on the table and order themselves; the order reaches the kitchen. Can also call the waiter. Added on top of AUZsPOS.

## AUZsPay
Payroll and attendance: staff clock in and out (with location check if wanted), leave, advances and loans, payslips, PF/ESI/professional tax settings, reports. Staff have their own simple login.

## AUZsLedger
Accounting with GST: sales and purchase bills, payments, GST reports, inventory valuation, bank statement import, scan a supplier invoice to fill a bill. Posting needs a connection; drafts can be written offline.

## AUZsMob
For mobile phone shops: stock by IMEI, purchases from vendors, sales with profit, repairs, dues, staff sales and profit sharing, reports for any period. Hindi and English. Works offline.

## AUZslab Salon
A salon's own website with booking, customers, bills, staff logins and a gallery, in the client's own colours and logo.

## Website Builder
Drag-and-drop website editor for a client's own site.

## Included modules
CRM, billing and invoicing and inventory are part of AUZsPOS (and Salon). They are not sold separately.

## Custom build (AUZslab Studio)
A specially designed system built to the client's demand. Scope, quote and payment stages are agreed in writing first. No fixed price list.
